Privacy Policy

Luminar: Endless Night · Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. Controller

This game is operated by Crofthen (Diego Marín), based in Chile. For any privacy question contact nyandiego@gmail.com.

2. What data the game asks for

The game does not ask for an account, name, email, phone number or location, has no chat or social features, and does not send your progress to any server of ours.

3. Data stored on your device

Your progress (Embers, talents, characters, zones, records, settings, language, items, skins and the record of purchases already received) is stored only on your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads with Google AdMob. To show ads and measure their performance, AdMob may collect your device’s advertising ID, device information (model, Android version, language), your IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred) and ad interaction data. Google processes that data under its policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Consent. Where the law requires it (for example, the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent using Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can change it at any time in Settings → Privacy inside the game.

Personalized ads. You can turn them off or reset your advertising ID on your Android phone: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your version) and choose “Delete advertising ID” or “Reset advertising ID”.

Rewarded ads are always optional and never appear during gameplay. The “Remove ads” purchase removes full-screen ads.

5. In-app purchases

Optional purchases (Ember packs, Starter Pack, premium skins and remove ads) are processed by Google Play. The game does not receive or store your card or payment details; it only receives from Google the purchase receipt (product ID and a purchase code) to deliver what you bought, avoid delivering it twice and let you restore your purchases. That receipt is stored only on your device. Refunds are handled by Google Play.

6. Analytics and diagnostics

The game does not use third-party analytics (no Firebase or similar) nor its own crash-reporting tools. It logs a few internal events (for example, opening the shop) only in the device’s memory during the session and does not send them anywhere. The only diagnostic data that leaves your device is what Google AdMob collects (section 4) and, if enabled in your Google account, Google Play’s performance and crash reports (Android vitals).

7. Children

The game is aimed at people over 13 years old and does not intentionally collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it and object to its processing, in accordance with Chilean personal data protection law (Law No. 19,628 and, once in force, Law No. 21,719) and, if you live in another country, the law that applies to you (for example, the GDPR in the European Union). Because the game stores your progress only on your device, you can delete it yourself by uninstalling the game or clearing its data. To exercise any right regarding data we process, or for questions about Google’s data, write to nyandiego@gmail.com; we will reply within a reasonable time. Data collected by Google AdMob is managed with Google’s controls (myadcenter.google.com).

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version at this same address and update the “Last updated” date. Important changes will be announced on the game’s store page.
